## Trade/crafting triage

- The notable tradeable stock is **86 Perfect Gems**. Best practical approach is to hold/use or sell in bundles rather than spend listing slots on individual low-value gems. Perfect Amethysts (13), Topazes (16), Diamonds (16), Emeralds (13), Skulls (11), Sapphires (9), and Rubies (8) are the shown perfect-gem counts.
- Perfect Amethysts and Rubies are worth keeping for caster/blood crafting recipes; Perfect Skulls and other perfect gems are useful for reroll/crafting recipes. Confirm current recipe requirements and HC S14 trade demand before selling.
- Chipped through Flawless gems are primarily crafting/upgrading materials at these quantities. Cube-upgrade or bundle; no individual price assigned.
- **No live HC S14 market check was made**, so trade prices are intentionally unassigned. For the later spreadsheet, price the 86 perfect gems as counts by type and use a current bulk-listing check rather than assuming a fixed rune rate.
