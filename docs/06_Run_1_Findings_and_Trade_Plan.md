## Decision summary

### Best immediate self-use tests

1. **Throw Barbarian gloves:** test the Run 1 crafted Gauntlets in place of the current Heavy Bracers. They keep 3% life leech and add 20% IAS, 16% ED, +10% AR, +22 cold resistance, and +19 mana; the main visible loss is 13% MF and +109 flat AR. No life-leech loss, and cold resistance increases. Test in a safe area and retain the old gloves.
2. **Keep the Run 1 Mesh Belt and crafted Ring as a paired alternate, not a blind permanent swap.** Together with the new Gauntlets, replacing the Plated Belt, Heavy Bracers, and currently equipped corrupted ring produces a calculated delta of **+32 Fire / +10 Cold / −3 Lightning / +10 Poison** from the documented 41/80/75/50 stat line, or approximately **73/90/72/60**. This is based on transcribed item mods, not a new in-game comparison. It also changes life leech from **13% to 9%** while adding **6% mana leech**; keep the old ring/belt until tested. The package gives +20 IAS, +54 Life on the belt, and three-res coverage on the ring, but it does not cap every resistance.
3. **Mercenary safety plan:** the most promising Run 1 combination is **Tal Rasha's Horadric Crest (ethereal) + Smoke in the 2-socket Superior Wire Fleece**, while retaining **Purity + the current Sharp Arrows**. The merc already has 128 Strength, enough for the Wire Fleece's 111 requirement. Smoke uses **Nef + Lum**; the updated rune inventory has Nef ×6 and Lum ×4, leaving Nef ×5 and Lum ×3 after the craft. PD2's chest-runeword table gives Smoke +20% FHR and a variable +30–50 all resistances. Tal's Crest adds +16 all res on this roll, +56 Life, +28 Mana, +8% life leech and +7% mana leech. If the current Sigon's 10% leech set line is active, replacing its helm/armor with Tal's/Smoke changes total life leech from about 24% to about 22% (bow 7 + arrows 7 + helm 8), while greatly improving resist coverage. Make Smoke first, then compare the actual merc stat screen; the Smoke resist roll is variable.
4. **Keep the current Throw Barbarian helm, Hawkmail armor, 24-all-res amulet, and equipped weapons for now.** The Run 1 Avenger Guard helm loses the current helm's +20 all-res and would worsen the Barbarian's fire/lightning/poison profile. Hawkmail's Cannot Be Frozen remains an important baseline; do not replace it with an armor runeword on the Barbarian until another CBF source is established.

### Mercenary items to keep as alternatives, not equip by default

- Keep **Purity Long Bow** for Prayer healing, and keep the current Sharp Arrows (7% life leech, Prevent Monster Heal, +29 poison res). Do not swap to Riphook or Ramfodder while the player relies on Purity; Ramfodder is a bolt quiver and is incompatible with the bow.
- **Swiftwind Needle Sharp Arrows** add damage, +34% FRW, +28 Dex, +50 cold res and +25 lightning res, but lose the current arrows' 7% life leech, PMH, poison resistance and mana-after-kill. They over-invest in the merc's already-high cold/lightning resists while lowering poison/leech utility. Hold for a controlled test only.
- **Blunt Arrows** add 188–268 fire damage, poison damage, 20% Open Wounds, 241 OW damage/sec, +20 lightning res, MF and GF, but also lose leech/PMH/poison resistance. They are an offensive experiment, not a safe default.
- The ethereal Sharktooth Armor can be socketed for 3 sockets and could make Treachery (Shael–Thul–Lem), but Treachery is an offensive alternative, not the safer first choice for this merc's current fire/cold resistance gaps. Keep the base until the Smoke/Tal's comparison is complete.
