## Practical gear and sell triage

1. **Do not treat the corrupted Sharkskin Boots as an automatic safety upgrade.** Against the documented Chain Boots, they net +14 cold / +38 poison / +40 FRW, but lose +32 lightning; from the current 41/80/75/50 stat line lightning would fall to about 43. Against Infernostride they trade +30 fire/+5 max fire for poison and mobility. Only use as a zone-specific test after checking live resists; see [`06_Run_1_Findings_and_Trade_Plan.md`](06_Run_1_Findings_and_Trade_Plan.md).
2. **Jawbone Cap is worth a controlled comparison** against the crafted Fanged Helm. It grants +2 Throwing Mastery, +2 Shout, +2 Double Swing and 6% mana leech, but gives up +20 all-res and 27% CB. Since fire/poison are already the resist gaps, do not equip it in Hell unless charms/other gear keep the required resists safe.
3. **Amulet conflict / possible duplicate:** the Sortables screenshot shows a Nokozan Relic with 29% FHR, +7% max fire res and 24% fire res. The Unique Items transcription previously recorded another Nokozan with 26% FHR, +6% max fire res and 34% fire res. Treat these as separate observed copies/rolls until stash screenshots confirm whether one was duplicated or misread; compare the actual item before changing the earlier transcription.
4. **Merc quiver test:** the Sortables Sharp Arrows offer raw ED/max/cold damage, while Ramfodder offers IAS/FHR/knockback/−2% enemy physical resistance but is a bolt quiver. Keep Mahala's currently equipped Purity + Sharp Arrows baseline. Ramfodder is not compatible with the bow; compare it only after a Purity crossbow is crafted/equipped and Prayer healing is verified.
5. **Weapon-test note:** the current pair is player-reported as Lacerator + Wraith Flight. Great Pilum and the recorded 343% ED / 23% CB Simbilan are possible comparisons only; the old Simbilan/Fool's Francisca setup is no longer the reported active pair. Keep rollback weapons until safe runs establish the new pair's damage, speed and Amp uptime.
6. **Trade-site priorities from this tab:** (a) corrupted Sharkskin Boots, (b) Assassin Traps skiller with fire damage, (c) 15 IAS / 4 poison-skill jewel, (d) LLD 14/6 damage +6 Strength +11 MF jewel, (e) 10 FCR dual-res ring, (f) Jawbone Cap. The recorded Paladin Combat skiller may have been traded for 15 WSS; identity/remaining quantity is unconfirmed, so don't list it as owned until verified. Validate active HC buyers first; nearly every estimate here is LOW confidence and many pieces are better bundled or vendored.
7. **Elite crafting-base note:** no blank elite throwing-weapon base is shown. The ethereal Diamond Mail has affixes already and is not a clean base; the Great Pilum and Simbilan are already crafted/modified items, not fresh bases.
